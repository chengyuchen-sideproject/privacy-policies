# Privacy Policy — 說錢 SpeakCoin

_Last updated: 2026-08-15_

English | 繁體中文在下方 ↓

SpeakCoin is an offline personal expense tracker. The short version: **nothing
leaves your phone.**

## What data the app handles

- **The entries you make.** Amount, category, date, an optional note, and
  whether you marked the entry as essential. Stored in a private database on
  your device.
- **Budgets and saving goals** you choose to set.
- **The text of what you said**, when you add an entry by voice, kept so you can
  check what was heard.

## What the app does NOT do

- **No internet.** The app declares **no `INTERNET` permission**. It is
  technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.
- **No servers, no cloud, no accounts, no sync.**
- **No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.**
- **No selling or sharing** of any data with anyone.

## Speech recognition

Voice entry uses Android's built-in speech recogniser. The audio is handled by
your device's system speech service (typically Google's, depending on your
phone), under that service's own privacy policy — not by this app. SpeakCoin
receives only the resulting text and never stores or transmits the audio itself.
Where your phone has an on-device speech model installed, recognition does not
leave the device at all.

## Permissions used

- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`)** — only while you are recording an entry by
  voice. No background listening; the app has no service that could do so.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)** — used only to warn you when a budget
  you set is close to its limit.

## Where data is stored & how long

In SpeakCoin's private database on your device. It stays until you delete the
entries or uninstall the app, which removes everything. Moving to a new phone
starts fresh unless you export a backup yourself.

## Backups you export

The backup and CSV export features write a file to the location **you** pick.
Once written, that file is outside the app and under your control — if you save
it to a cloud folder, that cloud provider's terms apply to it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data online.

## Changes

Any future change to this policy will be published at this URL with an updated
date.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at
<https://github.com/chengyuchen-sideproject/privacy-policies/issues>.

# 隱私權政策 — 說錢 SpeakCoin

_最後更新：2026-08-15_

說錢是一款**離線的個人記帳 App**。一句話：**所有資料都不離開你的手機。**

## App 會處理的資料

- **你記下的每一筆**：金額、分類、日期、選填備註，以及你是否標記為「必要」。
  存在裝置內的私有資料庫。
- **你設定的預算與存錢目標**。
- **語音記帳時你說的那句話的文字**，保留下來讓你可以核對聽到的內容。

## App 不會做的事

- **無網路**：App **未宣告 `INTERNET` 權限**，技術上根本無法把資料傳到任何地方。
- **無伺服器、無雲端、無帳號、無同步。**
- **無分析、無廣告、無追蹤、無第三方 SDK。**
- **不販售、不分享**任何資料給任何人。

## 語音辨識

語音記帳使用 Android 內建的語音辨識。**音訊是交由你手機的系統語音服務處理**
（通常是 Google 的，視手機而定），適用該服務自己的隱私權政策，不是本 App 的。
說錢只拿到辨識後的文字，不會儲存或傳送音訊本身。如果你的手機裝了裝置端語音模型，
辨識過程完全不離開手機。

## 使用的權限

- **麥克風（`RECORD_AUDIO`）**：只在你按下語音記帳、實際錄音的那段期間使用。
  不會背景監聽——這個 App 沒有任何可以背景錄音的服務。
- **通知（`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`）**：只用於在你自己設定的預算快超支時提醒你。

## 資料儲存位置與保留時間

存在說錢於裝置內的私有資料庫。會保留到你自己刪除或解除安裝 App（會刪除全部）為止。
換新手機即重新開始，除非你自己匯出備份。

## 你自己匯出的備份

備份與 CSV 匯出會把檔案寫到**你指定的位置**。檔案一旦寫出就在 App 之外、由你自己管理
——如果你存到雲端資料夾，就適用該雲端服務的條款。

## 兒童

本 App 非針對兒童設計，且不在線上收集任何個人資料。

## 變更

日後政策若有變更，會在此網址更新並附上新日期。
